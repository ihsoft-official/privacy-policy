Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

Welcome to Metrimonial ("we," "our," or "us"). Respecting and protecting your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Summary: Metrimonial is designed exclusively for adult Muslims seeking serious marriage (Nikah). We do not sell your personal data. All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized matrimonial matchmaking, we collect the following types of information when you create an account or interact with the app:

  • Account & Basic Profile Information: Full name, email address, phone number, gender, date of birth, age, city/country of residence, and profile picture.
  • Matrimonial Attributes: Religion, sect, marital status, education level, profession, caste/community background, height, family background, and partner expectations.
  • User Content: Profile bio, gallery photos, and in-app chat messages exchanged with mutually accepted matches.
  • Device & Technical Identifiers: Device ID, Advertising ID (IDFA / GAID for AdMob ads), IP address, operating system, and app crash logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate application functionality, including:

  • Matchmaking & Personalization: To display your candidate profile to compatible matches based on preferences (age, city, education, sect, profession).
  • Account Security & Verification: To verify user identity via email authentication and protect our community against fake or fraudulent accounts.
  • Communication Services: To enable interest request delivery and secure in-app messaging between mutually connected members.
  • Subscription Processing: To manage auto-renewable premium memberships via Google Play Billing and Apple App Store In-App Purchases.
  • Advertising & Analytics: To deliver non-intrusive advertisements (for free tier users) and analyze performance using Google Mobile Ads and Firebase Analytics.

3. Information Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Information is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database (Firestore), authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud functions infrastructure.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Serving banner and interstitial ads to free-tier users.
  • Apple App Store & Google Play Store: Processing in-app subscription payments and entitlement verification.

4. In-App Chat Privacy & Community Safety

To maintain a dignified, respectful, and family-friendly environment:

  • Chat functionality requires mutual acceptance of an interest request before messaging is unlocked.
  • In-app privacy filters block unsolicited phone numbers and email addresses in chat messages to prevent spam and off-platform abuse.
  • Users can block or report any profile at any time directly within the app.

5. Data Security

All data exchanged between the Metrimonial app and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 transport layer security. Administrative controls and strict Firestore security rules restrict unauthorized database access.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You retain full control over your personal data:

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated profile data at any time via Settings -> Account Deletion inside the app.
  • Data Removal Request: You may also request complete removal of your data by contacting our support team at ih7063768@gmail.com. Upon account deletion, your profile, photos, chat history, and request logs are permanently purged from our active database.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Metrimonial is strictly intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 18. If we discover that a minor has created an account, the account and data will be terminated immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
