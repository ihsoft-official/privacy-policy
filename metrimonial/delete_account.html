Delete Your Metrimonial Account

You can request permanent deletion of your Metrimonial account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Metrimonial account and associated personal data, send an email to our support team.

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Metrimonial account and include the following:

  • Your Metrimonial account email address
  • A clear request to delete your account

Contact Metrimonial Support

Email us and our support team will process your account deletion request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, the following account-associated personal data will be deleted where applicable:

  • Profile information
  • Profile photos
  • Education and profession information
  • Location and matchmaking preferences
  • Interest requests associated with your account
  • Account-related personal information

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate purposes, including legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other obligations required by applicable law.

Any information retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary or as required by applicable law.

Important: Account deletion is intended to permanently remove your account and associated personal data. Some information may remain where retention is legally required or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

Processing Time

We will review and process your account deletion request within a reasonable period. Once the deletion process has been completed, we will notify you by email.
