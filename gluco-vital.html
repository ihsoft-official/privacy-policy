Privacy Policy — Gluco Vital

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Gluco Vital ("we", "our", or "us") is a lifestyle health tracking application that allows users to log personal wellness data such as blood sugar readings, blood pressure readings, and related health entries. The app also provides charts, insights, and downloadable reports for personal record keeping and sharing with healthcare professionals.

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Gluco Vital.

1. Information we collect

Gluco Vital may collect and process the following types of information depending on the features you use:

  • User-entered health data — blood sugar readings, blood pressure readings, timestamps, notes, and other wellness information that you manually enter into the app.
  • Account information — if sign-in is enabled, basic authentication information such as an email address or user ID may be processed through Firebase Authentication.
  • App usage and diagnostic data — information such as app interactions, feature usage, crash reports, and performance data may be collected to help us understand and improve the application.
  • Device and advertising information — when advertising services are enabled, third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob may collect information including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertising information, performance information, and interactions with advertisements.

We do not sell your personal health information or use your health readings for advertising purposes.

2. How we use your data

We may use collected information to:

  • Store and sync your records across devices
  • Generate charts and personal insights from your entries
  • Create downloadable reports for personal records or sharing
  • Provide authentication and account-related functionality
  • Improve app performance, stability, and user experience
  • Display and measure advertisements within the application
  • Provide relevant or personalized advertising where permitted

3. Advertising

Gluco Vital uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements within the app and help measure advertising performance.

Google AdMob may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertising data, performance information, crash information, and interactions with advertisements. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, measurement, and improving advertising performance.

Depending on your device settings, permissions, location, applicable laws, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Gluco Vital does not use your blood glucose readings, blood pressure readings, medical records, or other health information that you enter into the app to personalize advertisements.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, Gluco Vital may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to access the device's advertising identifier and support personalized advertising and advertising measurement.

If you grant permission, applicable advertising partners may use the device advertising identifier for advertising and measurement purposes. If you deny permission, the advertising identifier is not made available to the Google Mobile Ads SDK for its ad requests.

You can manage or change your tracking permission at any time through your device's privacy settings.

5. Data storage and security

User data may be securely stored using Firebase and Google Cloud services. We apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data transmitted between the app and our services is protected using encryption in transit. Access to account-related information is restricted according to applicable authentication and security controls.

6. Third-party services

Gluco Vital may use third-party services to provide authentication, analytics, crash reporting, cloud services, and advertising. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

  • Firebase Authentication — used for authentication and account management.
  • Firebase Analytics — used to understand app usage and improve the application.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — used to identify and diagnose crashes and stability issues.
  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Information about Google's privacy practices is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. App permissions

Gluco Vital requests permissions only when required for specific app functionality. Depending on the features used, permissions may include internet/network access, notification access, file or storage access for exporting reports, and advertising/tracking permission on supported iOS devices.

8. Reports and data export

The app allows users to generate personal reports in formats such as PDF or CSV summarizing logged information. These reports are generated within the app and are shared only when the user chooses to export or share them.

9. Health information and advertising

Health information entered into Gluco Vital, including blood sugar and blood pressure readings, is used to provide the app's health tracking, charts, calculations, and reporting features.

We do not intentionally use user-entered health information to create advertising profiles, personalize advertisements, or target users with health-related advertisements.

10. Children's privacy

Gluco Vital is intended for general wellness tracking and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. Your data rights and choices

You have control over your information and may:

  • View your stored records at any time
  • Edit or delete your health entries
  • Export or share your reports
  • Delete your account and associated account data
  • Manage advertising and tracking permissions through your device settings

12. Data retention

Your account and associated information may be retained while your account remains active or as necessary to provide the requested services.

If you delete your account, we will take reasonable steps to delete associated account data within a reasonable timeframe, subject to applicable legal, security, backup, and operational requirements.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, our services, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices. Changes will be reflected in this document with an updated effective date.
